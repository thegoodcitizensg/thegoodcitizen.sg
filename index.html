About · The Good Citizen · Your role model photographer

Why The Good Citizen?

Most of us remember growing up with lessons on how to be a good citizen — or 好公民.

They were simple, yet valuable principles: be considerate, take responsibility, do the right thing even when no one is watching. Those lessons stayed with me.

At a wedding, being a Good Citizen looks a little different — it means moving quietly, anticipating moments before they happen, and carrying the weight of the day so the couple doesn’t have to.

A wedding is more than an event to be captured. It’s a day filled with nerves, expectations, family dynamics, and meaning. Our role isn’t to manufacture moments or pull you out of them — it’s to protect them.

We work calmly, intentionally, and with care for the people in the room.

So you can stay present.
So you can breathe.
So you can remember not just how the day looked, but how peaceful it felt.

And yes — having stood at the altar ourselves, we understand the anticipation, the weight, and the quiet prayers that come with saying “I do.”

This is what The Good Citizen stands for: showing up faithfully, doing the work well, and serving the moment with integrity.

Meet the Good Citizens

TIMO

The OG Good Citizen, Timo is the founder and principal photographer at the brand. Having shot images for half his life, and weddings for a large fraction of that, speed and quality is the guarantee he brings to the table.

When not out shooting or sitting at the table blitz-editing your images back to you in record time, you can find Timo working with AI tools to create apps that make life better!

And because Timo edits fast and delivers faster, you'll be scrolling through your gallery while your flowers are still alive! Pre-weddings and family sessions come back by the next day. Weddings, by the next week. That's his honest guarantee!

STEPH

Stephanie, our trusty associate, radiates beauty inside-out. Tested and proven with clients coming back with raving reviews, Steph is our Family Time Capsule expert, cause who else but a mum would know how to get a family to dance in the palm of her hands?!

Steph is a bonafide good citizen as she's also involved in many nonprofit organizations besides being a proud wife, mum to one (so far) and pawrent to two.

Privacy Policy — WeatherOrNot

Last Updated: May 16, 2026

Introduction

WeatherOrNot ("we", "our", or "us") is a weather app designed for photographers in Singapore. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect — Information You Provide

No Account Required: WeatherOrNot does not require registration or login. We do not collect any personal identifiable information.

Automatically Collected Information

Coarse Location Data: We collect your approximate location (city/region level, not GPS coordinates) to provide local weather forecasts and rain radar data. Location access is requested on first launch. Device Information: We collect limited device information (device type, operating system version) for advertising purposes through Google AdMob. Advertising ID: Your device's advertising identifier is used to serve relevant advertisements.

How We Use Your Information

Weather Services: Providing accurate weather forecasts, rain radar, and golden hour predictions for Singapore. Location Services: Determining your region to show relevant local weather data. Advertising: Displaying non-intrusive banner ads through Google AdMob. Analytics: Understanding app usage to improve user experience.

Data Storage

No Data Storage: We do NOT store your location data, weather preferences, or any personal information on our servers. Local Cache: Weather data is temporarily cached on your device for faster loading. This cache is cleared when you close the app. No Cloud Sync: Your data is NOT synced to the cloud or our servers.

Third-Party Services — Singapore National Environment Agency (NEA)

Purpose: Fetching official Singapore weather data (forecasts, rain radar, temperature). Data Shared: None (we only receive weather data). Privacy: NEA data is publicly available.

Third-Party Services — Google AdMob

Purpose: Displaying banner advertisements. Data Collected: Device advertising ID, device type, operating system. Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Ads Shown: Banner ads only (no interstitials or pop-ups). Personalization: We use non-personalized ads only. Ad Strategy: Light and non-intrusive (photographer-friendly).

Location Data

Why we need location: WeatherOrNot requires approximate location to show you relevant Singapore weather data and local rain conditions. How we use location: Determining your region within Singapore; Showing localized weather forecasts; Displaying rain radar for your area; Calculating golden hour times for your location. Location privacy: We use COARSE location only (not GPS precision); We do NOT store your location on our servers; We do NOT track your movement history; We do NOT share your location with advertisers; Location is only used for weather data retrieval. You can control location access: iOS Settings → WeatherOrNot → Location → While Using the App (recommended).

Data Sharing

We do NOT: Sell your personal information or location data; Share your location with third parties (except NEA API for weather data); Store your location history; Track your movements or create location profiles; Collect personal identifiable information (name, email, phone); Access your contacts, camera, photos, or other sensitive permissions.

Children's Privacy

WeatherOrNot is suitable for users of all ages (4+). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app requires location access but does not store location data or collect any personal information.

Your Rights

How do I log a session?

How do programs work?

The app generates a periodized block from your 1RMs, days per week, and weeks to your meet — with automatic deloads and a peaking taper. Coaches can also hand-build a program lift by lift. Tap a program day to start logging it with the prescribed work pre-filled.

How do I link with my coach (or athlete)?

What does my coach see?

While you're linked: your sessions (including reps, loads, and makes/misses), your stats and personal bests, your upcoming meet, your training status, and any notes you exchange. Your coach can assign you programs and — if you're mid-session — watch your training live. Unlinking stops all of this immediately.

What is the Sinclair score?

Sinclair lets lifters of different bodyweights compare total (snatch + clean & jerk). We use the IWF 2021–2024 coefficients, and show a Masters-adjusted figure if you're 35+.

Does my data leave my device?

Your training is stored on your device. When you sign in it also syncs to your account, and if you link a coach it's shared with them. If you never link a coach, nothing is shared. See our Privacy Policy for full detail.

Settings → Danger zone → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and all your cloud data (profile, stats, programs, sessions, notes, links) and erases your on-device training. It cannot be undone.

No for your own logging — it works offline. Coaching features (linking, program sync, live view, notes) need a connection.
